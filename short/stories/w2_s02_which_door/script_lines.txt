Which door would you pick?
Noah stood in a hallway with three doors.
A red door, a blue door, and a green door.
A sign said, "Pick one."
Noah is not a good decision maker.
He picked red.
Behind the red door was a mountain of pillows.
Noah dove in.
He sank all the way to the bottom.
It took him ten minutes to climb out.
Then he picked blue.
Behind the blue door was a giant trampoline.
Noah bounced once.
Twice.
Three times.
He bounced right out of the house.
He landed on the roof, upside down.
He walked back inside, dizzy.
Only one door was left.
Green.
Noah took a deep breath.
He opened it slowly.
Behind it sat a goat in a tiny suit, at a tiny desk.
The goat looked up.
You're hired.
