# Week 1 scripts (about 29 s each; not recorded yet, pending approval)
1 Mon Horror "Name From Outside": Your mom is calling your name from outside. Don't answer. In this town, nobody answers a voice from outside. Noah heard his mom at the window. Perfect voice. Every word. "Noah, open up." He took one step. Then his real mom whispered from downstairs, "Don't answer it." The voice outside went silent. Then it spoke again, in her exact whisper. It had been learning her through the walls. The window handle turned. Noah held his breath. It said his name once more. Softly. And this time it sounded like his own.
2 Tue Comedy "Noah vs The Cactus": Noah picked up the cactus. Big mistake. He thought it was soft. It was not soft. He tried a glove. The glove lost. He tried a spoon. The spoon lost. He tried a very large pillow. The cactus kept the pillow. By now, the whole street was watching. Noah took a deep breath. He grabbed it with both hands. And held on. Nothing happened. He had done it. He picked up a cactus. He looked at his hands. Then at the cactus. Then at his hands again. It was plastic. Noah did not speak for the rest of the day.
3 Wed Relatable "Ten Minutes": Mom will be home in ten minutes. The kitchen is on fire. Noah grabs a towel. The towel catches fire. He grabs a bucket. Empty. He opens the freezer, grabs all the ice, and throws it in. The fire is out. The kitchen is a lake. Eight minutes. He grabs a mop. Five minutes. He hides the burnt towel. Two minutes. He sits at the table with a book. The door opens. Mom looks at the floor. Noah looks at Mom. "Why is it wet?" "I... cleaned." She smiles. "Good boy." Behind her, the toaster starts to smoke.
4 Thu Mystery "One Inch": Every morning, one thing in his room moves exactly one inch. First the lamp. Then the chair. Then the poster. Noah drew chalk lines around everything. Tuesday, the lamp was one inch left of its line. Wednesday, the bed. So he set up a camera. Thursday morning, he watched the footage. At 3 a.m., his own door opened. Noah walked in, fast asleep. He moved the lamp, one inch, and walked out. He stared at the screen. Then he saw the corner of the room. Another Noah stood there. Awake. Watching him. Waving. Noah slowly looked behind him.
5 Fri Twist "Free Pizza": Free pizza. But every slice made him smaller. Noah won a pizza from the new shop. One slice. Nothing happened. Two slices. His shoes felt loose. Three slices. The table looked taller. He kept eating. It tasted too good. Five slices. His shirt was a tent. Seven slices. The last one was bigger than him. He climbed it anyway. The owner leaned down, smiling. "Welcome." Noah turned. The pizza box had a tiny door. It said "Staff Only." And a sign beside it said, "Now hiring. Small applicants only."
6 Sat Horror "Count The Stairs": Never count the stairs at night. In this town, every staircase has thirteen steps. Noah counted twelve. He counted again. Twelve. He went back up and counted down. Fourteen. The stairs were changing. On the last count, he felt the bottom step breathe. He ran upstairs and stopped. His room was gone. Only stairs now, going up forever. The extra step was right behind him. It said, "You counted." Noah slowly turned his head. The step below him was smiling. And it was already counting him back.
7 Sun Wholesome twist "The Snowman": The snowman was taller every morning. Noah built him knee-high. Monday, waist-high. Tuesday, as tall as the roof. His little sister loved him. Noah did not. Wednesday night, he watched from the window. At midnight, the snowman leaned over the house and held out his arms. A huge storm swept across the street. In the morning, every house was buried in snow. Except theirs. Noah ran outside. The snowman had melted into a small puddle. Beside it lay a carrot and two buttons. Noah picked them up. "Thank you," he said.
