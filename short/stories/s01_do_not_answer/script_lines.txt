Do not answer when it calls your name.
In this town, everyone knows that rule.
Nobody explains why.
Noah was still in the yard when the sun went down.
A voice called from the fence.
It sounded exactly like his mom.
Noah froze.
In the kitchen window, his mom stirred a pot, lips closed.
The voice called again.
Same words. Half a second late.
Noah backed toward the house.
The gate swung slowly open.
Nothing stood behind it. Only the dark, listening.
A third time. Softer. Closer.
He finally understood the rule.
It can only repeat what it has heard.
And anything you answer, it keeps.
Noah covered his mouth.
Behind the fence, something stood far too tall.
Its head tilted.
The back door opened.
"Noah?" his little sister whispered.
The dark answered for him.
Coming.
