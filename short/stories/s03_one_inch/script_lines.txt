Something moved one inch every night.
First, Noah's lamp.
Then his chair.
Then his shoes by the door.
One inch. Never more.
Noah told his little sister.
She shrugged. "Maybe it's a ghost."
Noah did not believe in ghosts.
So he set a trap.
A camera on the bookshelf, facing the room.
He checked the lock twice.
He checked the window twice.
Then he went to sleep.
In the morning, his lamp had moved.
His chair had moved.
His shoes had moved.
Even the rug had moved.
Noah pressed play.
For an hour, nothing happened.
Then Noah sat up.
His eyes were closed.
He walked slowly across the room.
He nudged the lamp one inch.
Then the chair.
Then the shoes.
Then the rug.
He walked back to bed and lay down.
Noah stared at the screen for a long time.
In the corner of the frame, a goat in a little shirt was watching him too.
Sleep well.
