One slice. Then Noah got smaller.
He had won a free pizza.
The box said, "Eat all eight slices."
Noah was not a careful reader.
He ate the first slice.
Nothing happened.
He ate the second slice.
His pants felt loose.
By the third, his shoes were too big.
By the fourth, his shirt was a tent.
By the fifth, the pizza looked as big as a bed.
He thought about stopping.
But the cheese smelled so good.
By the sixth, the table was a mountain.
By the seventh, he needed a ladder to reach the crust.
He climbed, slipped, and tried again.
His little sister walked in.
She looked at the empty plate.
She looked at the box.
She looked around the room.
"Noah?" she said.
He waved from the crust.
She picked him up between two fingers.
"You missed the small print," she said.
Slice eight was missing.
A goat in a chef's hat held it up.
Extra cheese?
