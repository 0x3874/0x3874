Can you pick up the cactus?
Noah said yes.
Noah was wrong.
First, bare hands.
That lasted one second.
Then oven mitts.
The cactus poked straight through.
Then a snow shovel.
The cactus slid off and landed in his hat.
Then he built a tiny crane out of a coat hanger.
It almost worked.
Then it wobbled.
Then it tipped.
Then Noah ran.
Every neighbor was watching from their windows.
Noah sat on the grass and sighed.
His little sister walked up with a tray.
"Why don't you ask it nicely?"
Noah rolled his eyes.
"Please," he said.
The cactus did not move.
Then a goat in a green costume stood up.
It had been the cactus the whole time.
Noah stared.
The goat winked.
Nice try.
