When your phone hits one percent.
That is Noah right now.
He has one message to send.
He turns the brightness all the way down.
He turns off the Wi-Fi.
He turns off the Bluetooth.
He turns off the lights.
He turns off the lights in the whole house.
He climbs onto the roof to get one bar of signal.
He drags the charger cable behind him.
His little sister watches from the window.
"What are you doing?" she asks.
"Saving my battery," he says.
He types the message very slowly.
"Can you bring a charger?"
He holds his breath.
He presses send.
The screen goes dark.
His sister points at the phone.
"Look at the corner."
A tiny lightning bolt sits next to the one percent.
It had been charging the whole time.
Noah looks at the dark house.
He looks at the cable.
A goat in sunglasses gives him a thumbs up.
Fully charged.
