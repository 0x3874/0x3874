In this town, everyone is home before the last lamp is lit.
Nobody asks why.
Last winter, a boy stayed out.
Now only his cap is left.
Eight minutes of light remain.
Everyone runs.
Shutters close. Doors lock.
But tonight, Noah's little sister is still outside.
He grabs a flashlight and goes after her.
Something tall steps out.
One step. Then another.
Noah looks back. It is closer.
He finds her under the last lamp.
Run!
He pounds on every door. Nobody answers.
The last lamp flickers, and dies.
The town wakes. Every door is open. Every house is empty.
The lamps were never for us.
They were its lock.
Noah turns to his own door.
It opens onto black.
It bends down.
You are late.
